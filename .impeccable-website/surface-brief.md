# Surface brief: Landing page

Visitor mode: **Persuade**

## Audience & job
Mixed independent travelers and couples/small groups booking Mekong slow boat routes on mobile. Primary job: choose a route, understand the leg, complete booking flow.

## Action & proof
- Primary action: Book a tour (placeholder payment, demo dates)
- Proof: TAT Licence 21/01279, route/itinerary clarity, meeting hubs from existing materials

## Direction
River navigation chart — cream chart paper, blue hydrography, yellow waypoint markers. Tours as chart legs, not generic card grid.

## Scope
Single scroll page at `/` with hero, 4 tour cards, detail sections, how-it-works, meeting points, FAQ, footer, booking sheet modal.

## Open
- Live payment provider
- Real photos
- Admin-managed availability
- Contact details in footer
